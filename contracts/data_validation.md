# Validation Contract

`report.json` records check ID, inputs, measured value, threshold, status, UTC, and provenance. G1 validates MOON_ME frame agreement; G2 validates Hermite interpolation; G3 validates topocentric Horizons vectors; G3b validates DSS-14/43/63 elevation; G4 validates browser/Python parity; G5-G11 cover generated products and science outputs.

G12 validates DEM unit/reference normalisation. G13 validates Scan staging latency and that stage-one GO results cannot become unlabelled NO-GO after bounded evaluation. G7 includes the CY-3 8.75-degree Sun-elevation anchor and qualitative mission timelines.
